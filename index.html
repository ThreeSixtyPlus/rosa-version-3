Questions about the packages

Are your prices per room?

Yes. Each package is priced per room, so you only pay for the spaces you want designed.

How long does design support last?

Essentials includes 3 weeks of support, Deluxe includes 6 weeks and Bespoke includes 8 weeks.

Do I need to measure the room myself?

We need some photographs and measurements of the space. Don't worry about the measuring, as we have a handy guide to show you how and what we need. If you'd rather we measured, add a site survey for £149.

What's in the shopping list?

Essentials includes a shopping list of 5 key items. Deluxe and Bespoke include a full shopping list for your scheme.

Not sure which package suits you?

Tell us about your room and we'll point you to the right one.
